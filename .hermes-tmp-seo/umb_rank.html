How this list is built
  • A world ranking, not a European one. Every federation counts — there is no “European players only” filter and no national-championship column. Column A is the World Championship; columns B–K are the World Cups.
  • Ten World Cups plus the World Championship — never more. The list keeps a rolling window of the 11 most recent counting events: the World Championship (column A) and the ten most recent World Cups (columns B–K). Each new World Cup takes the place of the oldest of the ten.
  • Absence penalties count. A player who was not entered in a counting tournament is given −8, and one who was entered but did not play −16. Both appear as a negative cell in that tournament’s column and are subtracted from the total — so a total can be negative.
  • Columns that do not count stay empty. A player only carries points in the events they actually played; every other column shows a dash, and the total on the right is the sum of their counting columns (penalties included).
