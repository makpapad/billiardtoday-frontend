How the process works — and where the platform can take over

Instead of updating the Excel by hand each time, the federations can enter their own national ranking directly:

1. The federation enters its ranking. The Luxembourg federation logs in at
   https://billiardtoday.com/federation/login
   and enters its national championship ranking for the season (category: 3-cushion individual) — the same list you sent me. The link is not in the website menu yet; I will add it.
2. You get an email. As soon as the federation submits, an automatic email goes to the CEB with the submission and a link to the review page. (While I test it, the emails come to my address.)
3. The submission is checked and approved. The review page is
   https://admin.billiardtoday.com/admin/ranking-review
   it shows the submission, the positions and the points the platform would give. Once approved, the platform recalculates and publishes the new version (V17) automatically at
   https://billiardtoday.com/rankings/ceb/3c-individual
   If something is wrong, the submission can be rejected with a short note and the federation receives an email explaining what to correct.
